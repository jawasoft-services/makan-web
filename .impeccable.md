## Design Context

### Users
Young adults (18-30) who eat food and have friends. They open the app to log what they ate, see what their friends had, and discover new spots through people they trust. They use Makan casually throughout the day — posting a quick photo at lunch, scrolling their friends' meals in the evening. No power users, no professionals, no content creators.

### Brand Personality
**Warm, honest, playful.** Like a friend showing you what they had for dinner. No pretense, no performance, a little fun. The brand celebrates ordinary food moments — your Tuesday night pasta in bad lighting is just as valid as a plated restaurant dish.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Raw and real, like BeReal/Locket — anti-polished, anti-influencer. But warm, not cold. The dark background (#050505) with orange accent (#FF9932) creates an intimate, evening-at-the-table feeling.
- **References**: BeReal, Locket — unpolished, authentic, anti-social-media energy
- **Anti-references**: Generic SaaS landing pages (stock photos, feature grids, "trusted by" logos), Instagram/TikTok polish, calorie-tracking clinical vibes
- **Theme**: Dark mode, warm orange accent, Plus Jakarta Sans
- **Photography**: Real meal photos from real users — no food styling, no filters

### Design Principles
1. **Real over perfect** — Rough edges are features, not bugs. Imperfect photos, casual copy, nothing that feels staged.
2. **Warm, not cool** — Orange tones, soft rounded corners, friendly type. Never cold/clinical/techy.
3. **Simple, not minimal** — The interface should feel effortless, but not empty. Every element earns its place by being useful or delightful.
4. **Friends, not followers** — Design for small groups, not audiences. Intimate, not broadcast.
5. **Joy, not optimization** — No guilt, no metrics, no "streaks broken." Food is fun.
