# Makan — Product Brief for Website Development
**Share what you eat. The food diary that remembers what you'd otherwise forget.**

## What Makan Is

**Makan is the food diary that remembers what you'd otherwise forget. Share what you eat with the friends who make it richer.**

Diary first, social network second. Every meal you photograph is yours first — a private archive of what you ate, where, and when. The friends who eat alongside you make it richer; they don't make it exist. The result: a food memory you'd actually keep, and a tiny food network you actually trust.

The name *makan* literally means *eat* in Indonesian (also Malay — both languages share an Austronesian root, and the brand is Indonesian-led). *"Sudah makan?"* — "have you eaten?" — is how people greet each other across Indonesia, Singapore, Malaysia, and Brunei. The brand name *is* the activity. In Anglophone markets, *makan* lands as a sticky loanword that becomes shorthand for the meal itself.

The lead verbs are **remember, capture, keep, look back** — not post, share, broadcast, viral.

## What Makan Is NOT

- Not a calorie counter or diet tracker
- Not a restaurant review platform (no star ratings)
- Not a food delivery app
- Not a recipe app
- Not "the new Instagram" or "Yelp for friends" — strangers' opinions are the enemy
- No nutritional data, no meal plans, no "healthy vs unhealthy" judgments
- No algorithms deciding what you see — your feed is your friends

## Core Ethos

**Trust your friends, not strangers. Remember, don't broadcast.**

Makan starts personal. The diary is yours first; the social layer is a gift, not a tax. Loss-framing — *"the meal you'd otherwise forget"* — lives in cold acquisition only; every in-funnel surface is gain-coded — the relief of having kept it. The arc is loss → relief.

Strangers' opinions are Yelp's lane, and Beli's. We compete by saying *"trust your friends, not strangers."* The friend graph is intimate by design — a small, trusted table, not a public square.

## The App — Feature by Feature

### 1. The Feed

Two views: **Friends** and **Public**.

Friends feed shows meals from people you follow — breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks. Each post has a photo, the meal name, where it was eaten (or "homemade"), the time of day, and reactions from friends.

Public feed shows meals from the wider Makan community. This is where you discover new food and new people.

Posts can be reacted to with emoji (yum face, heart eyes), commented on, shared, or bookmarked. There's no like count pressure — reactions are lightweight and joyful.

### 2. Posting a Meal

Tap the center "+" button. Take a photo or pick from your library. Tag the restaurant (Google Places search) or mark it as homemade. Add a caption, select the meal type (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snack), choose your audience (public, friends only, or private draft).

No filters. No editing tools beyond basic crop. Tuesday night pasta in bad lighting is exactly the meal worth keeping.

Posts work offline — they queue up and sync when you're back online.

### 3. Share Cards

When you share a meal outside Makan (to Instagram Stories, WhatsApp, iMessage), the app generates a branded share card — a clean, designed image with the food photo, your username, the meal name, and the Makan branding.

This is the primary growth engine. Every share card is a free ad for Makan in whatever country the sharer's followers live in.

### 4. Explore & Map Discovery

The Explore tab has two modes: **list** and **map**.

List view shows:
- **Hot Meals** — trending posts with the most engagement
- **Trending Craves** — dishes people are saving/craving
- **Top Spots** — restaurants ranked by meal count
- **Leaderboards** — users ranked by Makan Points, streaks, or badges
- **Popular Spots** — restaurant cards with photo carousels

Map view shows an Apple Maps overlay with restaurant pins. Tap a pin to see what your friends (and others) have eaten there. Pins show friend avatars so you can see at a glance where people you know have been.

Toggle between "Public" (all users) and "Friends" (only people you follow).

### 5. Profile & Food Diary

Your profile is your food diary. It shows:
- Profile photo, username, bio
- "Followed by [mutual friends]" social proof
- Current streak (consecutive days posting)
- Stats: total posts, friend count, cook type
- Foodie title and Makan Points progress bar
- Badge collection (0/100 earned)
- A grid of every meal you've kept

Other people's profiles show the same, with follow/friend request buttons.

### 6. Meal Calendar

A monthly calendar view where every day you posted shows meal thumbnails. Filter by meal type (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack). See your monthly stats: total meals, days active, unique spots visited.

The calendar doubles as a streak tracker — it visually shows your consecutive posting days with orange-highlighted dates. It's also the foundation for the annual *Year in Food* moment — the meals you kept this year, told as a story.

### 7. Streaks & Gamification

Makan uses gamification to build the daily diary habit:

**Streaks** — Keep at least one meal a day to keep your streak alive. Milestone celebrations at 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 days. Streak freezes (skip a day without breaking) and streak recovery (restore within 24 hours) available.

**Makan Points (MP)** — Earn points for keeping meals, tagging locations, adding captions, getting reactions, discovering new restaurants. Points contribute to your foodie tier.

**Foodie Titles (6 tiers):**
1. Curious Eater
2. Regular Foodie
3. Taste Explorer
4. Flavor Chaser
5. Food Connoisseur
6. Local Legend

Each tier has its own icon and color. Your current tier shows on your profile.

**Badges (100 achievements)** across categories: Journey, Explorer, Social, Creative, Seasonal, and Secret. Rarities range from Common to Legendary. A badge collection screen lets you browse locked/unlocked badges.

### 8. Friends & Social

- Send/accept/reject friend requests
- Follow/unfollow users (separate from friendship)
- View mutual friends
- Block/unblock users
- Friend suggestions ("People You Might Know")
- Tag friends in meal captions

### 9. Notifications

Two tabs: **Activity** (friend interactions — likes, comments, follows, tags) and **System** (announcements, reminders, gamification alerts).

Push notifications for: new likes, comments, friend requests, badge earned, title upgrade, streak milestones, streak at-risk warnings, and business nudges from restaurants.

### 10. Search

Search for users by username or restaurants by name. Recent searches are saved. Restaurant search uses Google Places for comprehensive coverage.

## Makan for Business (B2B Revenue Model)

This is Makan's primary revenue stream. Restaurants can claim their profile and access *customer memory analytics* — data on which dishes their customers have permanently kept in their food diaries.

**The pitch:**

> Yelp tells you what customers said once. Makan tells you what they remember. When 47 of your customers have your salmon dish in their permanent food diary — not as a one-time review, but as something they keep — that's not a Yelp comment. That's loyalty data Yelp can't access. **Memory is the moat.**

### How It Works

1. When Makan users keep meals and tag a restaurant, that restaurant accumulates memory data — who keeps coming back, how often, what dishes get kept (not just rated), what gets the most reactions from friends.

2. Restaurant owners can **claim** their Makan page for free. This gives them a verified badge and basic visibility into their posts.

3. Paid tiers unlock deeper insights and engagement tools.

### Business Tiers

**Free Claim**
- Verified badge on restaurant page
- See total visitor count
- See posts tagged at your restaurant
- Basic presence on the map

**Business Pro (paid monthly)** — *Social CRM for the diners who keep coming back to remember you.*
- Full visit frequency breakdown
- "Almost-regulars" segment (customers who visited twice but not a third time — the critical conversion point)
- Top dishes analysis (dishes most-kept by customers)
- Weekly engagement summary
- 14-day free trial

**Business Premium (paid monthly)**
- Everything in Pro
- Send "nudges" to almost-regulars (personalized messages with optional offers/rewards)
- Track nudge performance (who returned within 14 days)
- Visit trends over time
- Priority placement in discovery

### The "Almost-Regular" Insight

Research shows the third visit is where a customer becomes a regular. Makan identifies customers who visited twice but haven't come back — the "almost-regulars." This is the highest-value segment for any restaurant because one more visit converts them into a loyal repeat customer.

Business Pro shows you who these people are. Business Premium lets you reach out to them directly with a nudge.

### Business Dashboard

Restaurant owners get a dashboard showing:
- Profile views
- CTA link taps (menu URL, booking URL)
- New follows
- Meals kept by customers (a different signal than ratings)
- Visit metrics (by tier)
- Nudge performance (Premium)

### Restaurant Pages

Each restaurant has a page showing:
- Business details and amenities
- Menu URL and booking URL
- All meals kept there (photo grid)
- Friend indicators ("3 friends have kept meals here")
- Business verification badge

## Key Differentiators

1. **Diary first.** Every meal is yours first; friends make it richer. The product starts personal — there's no audience to perform for.

2. **The brand name is the moat.** *Makan* literally means *eat* across SEA's 280M+ people. *"Sudah makan?"* is how you say hello. No competitor can rebrand into that.

3. **Friend-powered, not stranger-powered.** You find restaurants through friends' actual kept meals — not paid placements, not algorithmic recommendations, not strangers' star ratings.

4. **Memory as B2B moat.** *"Yelp tells you what customers said once. Makan tells you what they remember."* Restaurants pay for memory data, not review data.

5. **Share cards as growth engine.** Every shared meal is branded. Growth is organic and global with zero marketing spend.

6. **Year in Food.** The annual nostalgic data drop — every meal you kept this year, told as a story. Spotify Wrapped, but for what you ate.

7. **Streaks without guilt.** The gamification is positive ("keep your flavor fire alive"), not punitive. Streak freezes and recovery prevent anxiety.

8. **38 languages from day one.** Full localisation means no friction for non-English speakers.
