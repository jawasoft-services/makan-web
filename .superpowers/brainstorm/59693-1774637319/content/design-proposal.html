Design Proposal: Makan Website Redesign

A dark, cinematic, air.inc-inspired redesign for the Makan beta waitlist. Review each section below.

Near-black base. Food photos provide all the color.

Typography

Hero headlines — 800 weight, tight tracking
Section headings
600 weight, natural tracking
Body copy for descriptions and explanations
400 weight, generous line-height (1.6)
Italic accent for emphasis words
Air.inc-inspired — sparingly on key words
Motion Philosophy
Scroll Reveal
Hero food photos cascade in on scroll. Framer Motion stagger animations. Elements fade-up as they enter viewport.
Ambient Motion
Feature tabs auto-rotate on ~8s interval. No jarring scroll-jacking. Smooth crossfades between tab content.
Micro-interactions
CTA hover glow. Meal card hover lift. FAQ accordion spring animation. Subtle, never distracting.
